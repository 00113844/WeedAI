# General Instructions:

# Resistance Warning:

FUHUA 540 is a member of the Glycines group of herbicides. FUHUA 540 has the inhibition of EPSP synthase mode of action. For weed resistance management FUHUA 540 is a Group 9 herbicide.

Some naturally occurring weed biotypes resistant to FUHUA 540 and other Group 9 herbicides may exist through normal genetic variability in any weed population. The resistant individuals can eventually dominate the weed population if these herbicides are used repeatedly. These resistant weeds will not be controlled by FUHUA 540 or other Group 9 herbicides. Since the occurrence of resistant weeds is difficult to detect prior to use, Fuhua Tongda accept no liability for any losses that may result from the failure of FUHUA 540 to control resistant weeds.
