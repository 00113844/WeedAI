# GENERAL INSTRUCTIONS

Organic matter (thatch/mat), phosphate levels and soil type will affect the degree of control of winter grass.

# Mixing

Mix concentrate with the required volume of water in the spray tank. Do not combine with other pesticides or fertilisers.

# Application

Exporsan may be applied with any spray equipment which provide uniform distribution. Calibrate equipment carefully and clean after use.

# RESISTANT WEEDS WARNING

Nuturf Exporsan Turf Herbicide is a member of the Organophosphorus group of herbicides. Nuturf Exporsan has the inhibitors of mitosis mode of action. For weed resistance management Nuturf Exporsan is a group E herbicide.

Some naturally occurring weed biotypes resistant to Exporsan and other inhibitors of mitosis herbicides may exist through normal genetic variability in any weed population. The resistant individuals can eventually dominate the weed population if these herbicides are used repeatedly. These resistant weeds will not be controlled by Exporsan or other Group E herbicides.

Since the occurrence of resistant weeds is difficult to detect prior to use, Nuturf accepts no liability for any losses that may result from the failure of Exporsan to control resistant weeds.
