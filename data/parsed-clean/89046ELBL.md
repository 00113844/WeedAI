# Resistance Warning:

RESISTANT WEEDS WARNING

GROUP J HERBICIDE

Titan Ethofumesate 500 SC Herbicide is a member of the Benzofuran group of herbicides.

Titan Ethofumesate 500 SC Herbicide is a herbicide with inhibitors of lipid synthesis (not AACase inhibitors) mode of action. For weed resistance management Titan Ethofumesate 500 SC Herbicide is a Group J herbicide. Some naturally-occurring weed biotypes resistant to Titan Ethofumesate 500 SC Herbicide and other Group J herbicides may exist through normal genetic variability in any weed population. The resistant individuals can eventually dominate the weed population if these herbicides are used repeatedly. These resistant weeds will not be controlled by Titan Ethofumesate 500 SC Herbicide or other Group J herbicides. Since the occurrence of resistant weeds is difficult to detect prior to use, Titan Ag Pty Ltd accepts no liability for any losses that may result from the failure of Titan Ethofumesate 500 SC Herbicide to control resistant weeds.
