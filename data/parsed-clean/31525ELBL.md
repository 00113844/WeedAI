# STORAGE AND DISPOSAL (5 L, 10 L and 20 L containers)

Store in a disposal pit specifically marked and set up for this purpose clear of waterways, desirable vegetation and tree roots. Empty containers and product should not be burnt. Do not re-use container for any other purpose.

# STORAGE AND DISPOSAL (110 L returnable containers)

Empty container by pumping through dry-break connection system. Do not attempt to breach the valve system or the filling point, or contaminate the container with water or other products. Ensure that the coupler, pump, meter and hoses are disconnected, triple rinsed and drained after each use. When empty, or contents no longer required, return the container to the point of purchase. This container remains the property of Bayer CropScience Pty Ltd.

# STORAGE AND DISPOSAL (1000 L minibulk container)

Store in the closed, original container in a cool, well-ventilated area. Do not store for prolonged periods in direct sunlight. Empty product as required into application equipment. Do not attempt to breach the valve. When the container is empty, close all caps and valves and return the container to the point of purchase.
