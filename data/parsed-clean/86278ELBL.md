# Restraints:

- DO NOT apply to Ornamentals under stress resulting from factors including (but not limited to) lack of moisture, hail damage, flooding, herbicide injury, mechanical injury, poor soil condition or disease.
- DO NOT broadcast over wet foliage.
- DO NOT apply in glasshouses or poorly ventilated poly-houses.
- DO NOT apply in windy conditions as likely to affect good distribution.
- DO NOT apply to putting greens.
- DO NOT apply via aerial application.
- DO NOT apply to turf grass within 2 weeks of mechanical disturbance such as aerification or verticutting.
- DO NOT apply to newly seeded turf areas.

# Resistance Warning:

GROUP D K HERBICIDE

FREEHAND® Herbicide is a member of the Dinitroanilines (DNAs) and Chloroacetamides groups of herbicides. The product has the combination of Inhibitors of microtubule assembly and inhibitors of cell division / inhibitors of very long chain fatty acids (VLCFA inhibitors) modes of action. For weed resistance management, the product is a Group D &#x26; a Group K herbicide. Some naturally occurring weed biotypes resistant to the product and other Group D &#x26; Group K herbicides may exist through normal genetic variability in any weed population. The resistant individuals can eventually dominate the weed population if these herbicides are used repeatedly. These resistant weeds will not be controlled by this product or other Group D or Group K herbicides.

Since the occurrence of resistant weeds is difficult to detect prior to use, BASF Australia Ltd accepts no liability for any losses that may result from the failure of this product to control resistant weeds.
