# Resistance Warning:

RESISTANT WEEDS WARNING

GROUP 14 HERBICIDE

Terrad’or® is a member of the Pyrimidindione group of herbicides. The mode of action of Terrad’or® is to inhibit protoporphyrinogen oxidase. For weed resistance management, Terrad’or® is a Group 14 Herbicide. Some naturally-occurring weed biotypes resistant to Terrad’or® and other Group 14 Herbicides may exist through normal genetic variability in any weed population. The resistant individuals can eventually dominate the weed population if these herbicides are used repeatedly. These resistant weeds will not be controlled by Terrad’or® or other Group 14 Herbicides.

Since the occurrence of resistant weeds is difficult to detect prior to use, Nufarm Australia Limited and FarmHannong Co Ltd accept no liability for any losses that may result from the failure of Terrad’or® to control resistant weeds. Strategies to minimize the risk of herbicide resistance are available. Contact your farm chemical supplier, consultant, local Department of Agriculture or Weedsmart (weedsmart.org.au).
