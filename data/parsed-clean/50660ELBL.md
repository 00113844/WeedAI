# CAUTION

# TAIPAN Herbicide

# KEEP OUT OF REACH OF CHILDREN

# READ SAFETY DIRECTIONS BEFORE OPENING OR USING

# DIRECTIONS FOR USE

| CROP | WEEDS      | RATE | COMMENTS                                                                                 |
| ---- | ---------- | ---- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| CR   | Weeds      | 2.0  | Aerial application may be applied by air (from a boom). Ensure the correct droplet size. |
| NSW  | Controlled | 12   | Results rapid after application. Apply 12 tools after harvester.                         |

# APPLICATION

Application should be made with a minimum water depth of 10 cm on the high side of the boom.

Water depth at application should be sufficient to ensure nozzles are submerged.

# GENERAL INSTRUCTIONS

Do not graze or cut for stock food for 7 weeks after application.

Control of starfruit may be required for optimum results.
