
# BARRICADE Pre-Emergent Herbicide HG

# Label Name

BARRICADE Pre-Emergent Herbicide HG

# Signal Headings

READ SAFETY DIRECTIONS BEFORE OPENING OR USING

# Constituent

ACTIVE CONSTITUENT: 480 g/L PRODIAMINE

# Statements

# Statement of Claims

For preventative control of weeds before weed germination in established lawns &#x26; turf, garden beds and potted plants.

- Long lasting preventative weed control for up to 6 months
- Can be used in a variety of situations
- Controls weeds before they become a problem

# Net Contents

100 mL – 5 L

# Restraints

- DO NOT apply to turf under stress, newly seeded, newly laid turf or sprigged turf. Delay application until turf is at 100% cover and established with a root system developed beyond 3 cm depth.
- DO NOT apply if heavy rain has been forecast within 48 hours or to waterlogged soil.
- DO NOT water or irrigate to the point of runoff within 3 days of application.
- DO NOT apply in vegetable gardens or around fruit trees or sensitive plants.
- DO NOT apply to garden beds over the top of mulch.
- DO NOT apply to recently re-potted or newly potted seeds, cuttings divisions or plants. Allow a period of establishment to allow a substantial root system to develop.
- DO NOT plant seeds in treated soil for 6 months after application.

# HOW TO PREPARE

Mix BARRICADE Pre-Emergent Herbicide as per the instructions below into a diluted mixture ready for application. If the diluted solution is left standing for some time, make sure you shake/agitate before use.

# Mixing – Garden Sprayer

1. Prior to opening, shake BARRICADE Pre-Emergent bottle well.
2. Add half the required amount of water to your garden sprayer.
3. Add the required quantity of BARRICADE Pre-Emergent to the half full garden sprayer.
4. Add the remaining water required and ensure sprayer is closed before agitating sprayer.
5. Only mix sufficient spray solution for application area and for the day of mixing.

# Mixing – Watering Can

1. Prior to opening, shake BARRICADE Pre-Emergent bottle well.
2. Add half the required amount of water to your watering can.
3. Add the required quantity of BARRICADE Pre-Emergent to your watering can.
4. Add the remaining water required and use the process to agitate the mixture.
5. Only mix sufficient spray solution for application area and for the day of mixing.

# HOW TO APPLY

# Lawn

- Measure the lawn area before application to determine the size and make up enough mixture to apply over the area.
- Evenly spray (or pour) the mixed solution over the area to be treated.
- Lightly water in, within 24 hours of application.
- Thoroughly clean all equipment after application. Empty and thoroughly rinse equipment such as sprayer, hoses, nozzles, and watering can with clean water.

# Garden beds and potted plants

- Measure the garden bed or potted plant area before application and make up enough mixture to apply to that area.
- Apply prior to weed emergence (remove existing weeds) and ensure product is placed as close to soil surface as possible. Ensure product is applied before mulching.
- Wash in immediately with a minimum of 3 mm rain or irrigation.
- Thoroughly clean all equipment after application. Empty and thoroughly rinse equipment such as sprayer, hoses, nozzles, and watering can with clean water.

# Precautions

- DO NOT allow spray or drift to contact recently planted or non-established plants, or vegetable gardens.
- DO NOT allow chemical containers or spray to get into drains, sewers, streams or ponds.
- DO NOT replant any crop, ornamentals or overseed with cool season grasses for winter cover to treated areas for a period of 6 months after application.
- DO NOT allow entry into treated areas until the spray has dried.
