# Resistance Warning

# RESISTANT WEEDS WARNING

GROUP 4 27 HERBICIDE

GENFARM PYRASULFOTOLE-MCPA EC HERBICIDE contains members of the pyrazole (pyrasulfotole) and phenoxy (MCPA) groups of herbicides. GENFARM PYRASULFOTOLE-MCPA EC HERBICIDE is a herbicide which inhibits 4-hydroxyphenylpyruvate dioxygenase (4-HPPD) and also acts by disruption of plant cell growth. For weed resistance management GENFARM PYRASULFOTOLE-MCPA EC HERBICIDE is a Group 4 and Group 27 herbicide. Some naturally-occurring weed biotypes resistant to GENFARM PYRASULFOTOLE-MCPA EC HERBICIDE, and other Group 4 and Group 27 herbicides, may exist through normal genetic variability in any weed population. The resistant individuals can eventually dominate the weed population if these herbicides are used repeatedly. These resistant weeds may not be controlled by GENFARM PYRASULFOTOLE-MCPA EC HERBICIDE or other Group 4 and Group 27 herbicides.

Since occurrence of resistant weeds is difficult to detect prior to use, Loveland Agri Products Pty Ltd accepts no liability for any losses that may result from the failure of GENFARM PYRASULFOTOLE-MCPA EC HERBICIDE to control resistant weeds.

Do not rely exclusively on GENFARM PYRASULFOTOLE-MCPA EC HERBICIDE for weed control. Use as part of an integrated weed management program involving herbicides with other modes of action and non-chemical methods of control. CropLife Australia resistance management strategies are available from your local agricultural chemical supplier. Refer to these strategies for details of how to manage the build up of resistant weeds on your farm.

# Precautions

Re-entry Period: Do not allow entry into treated areas until the spray has dried. When prior entry is necessary, wear cotton overalls buttoned to the neck and wrist (or equivalent clothing) and chemical resistant gloves. Clothing must be laundered after each day’s use.

# PROTECTION OF CROPS, NATIVE AND OTHER NON-TARGET PLANTS

DO NOT apply under weather conditions, or from spraying equipment, that may cause spray to drift and MCPA volatilization onto nearby susceptible plants/crops, cropping lands or pastures.

#
**Where GENFARM PYRASULFOTOLE-MCPA EC HERBICIDE at 2 L/ha is applied on alkaline soils, recropped areas that receive double rates (boom overlaps) may show increased symptoms of damage in crops such as canola, field pea, lentil, lupin, medic and vetch. This is generally restricted to discolouration (bleaching) of the crop but may also result in biomass reduction or reduced yields in some situations.
