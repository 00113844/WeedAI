# Resistance

# RESISTANT WEEDS WARNING

Warning: GROUP B HERBICIDE

Renege Herbicide is a member of the sulfonylurea group of herbicides. Renege Herbicide has the inhibitor of acetolactate synthase (ALS) mode of action. For weed resistance management Renege Herbicide is a Group B herbicide.

Some naturally occurring weed biotypes resistant to Renege Herbicide and other ALS herbicides may exist through normal genetic variability in any weed population. The resistant individuals can eventually dominate the weed population if these herbicides are used repeatedly. These resistant weeds will not be controlled by Renege Herbicide or other ALS herbicides.

Since the occurrence of resistant weeds is difficult to detect prior to use, Crop Culture accepts no liability for any losses that may result from failure of Renege Herbicide to control resistant weeds.

Large numbers of healthy surviving weeds can be an indication that resistance is developing. Efforts should be taken to prevent seed set of these survivors.

DO NOT use an ALS inhibitor herbicide against the same weed in the crop following the use of Renege Herbicide alone either as a fallow or pre-crop treatment or post emergent treatment.

Avoid the prolonged use of ALS inhibitor herbicides on the same weed population.

If the user suspects that an ALS inhibitor resistant weed is present, Renege Herbicide or other ALS inhibitors herbicides recommended for the control of that weed should not be used.

Strategies to minimise the risk of herbicide resistance are available. Consult your farm chemical supplier, consultant, local Department of Agriculture or Primary Industries, or local Crop Culture representative.

# Precautions:

DO NOT use chlorine bleach with ammonia. All traces of liquid fertiliser containing ammonia, ammonium nitrate or ammonium sulphate must be rinsed with water from the mixing and application equipment before adding chlorine bleach solution. Failure to do so will release a gas with a musty chlorine odour which can cause eye, nose and lung irritation. DO NOT clean equipment in an enclosed area.
