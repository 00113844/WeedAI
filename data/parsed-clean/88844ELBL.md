# Resistance Warning:

RESISTANT WEEDS WARNING

GROUP Z HERBICIDE

ProForce DODGER 100 SL Herbicide is a member of the Organoarsenic group of herbicides. DODGER 100 SL is a herbicide with diverse sites of action. For weed resistance management DODGER 100 SL is a Group Z herbicide. Some naturally occurring weed biotypes resistant to DODGER 100 SL and other Group Z herbicides may exist through normal genetic variability in any weed population. The resistant individuals can eventually dominate the weed population if these herbicides are used repeatedly. These resistant weeds will not be controlled by DODGER 100 SL or other Group Z herbicides. Since the occurrence of resistant weeds is difficult to detect prior to use, Indigo Specialty Products Pty Ltd accepts no liability for any losses that may result from the failure of DODGER 100 SL to control resistant weeds.
