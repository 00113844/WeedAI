# Resistance Warning

Warning: GROUP 6 12 HERBICIDE

CropSure Jagged Selective Herbicide is a member of the nitrile and nicotinanilide groups of herbicides. CropSure Jagged Selective Herbicide is an inhibitor of photo synthesis at photo system II and carotenoid biosynthesis. For weed resistance management, CropSure Jagged Selective Herbicide is a Group 6, 12 herbicide. Some naturally occurring weed biotypes resistant to CropSure Jagged Selective Herbicide and other Group 6, 12 herbicides may exist through normal genetic variability in any weed population. The resistant individuals can eventually dominate the weed population if these herbicides are used repeatedly. These resistant weeds will not be controlled by CropSure Jagged Selective Herbicide or other Group 6, 12 herbicides. Since the occurrence of resistant weeds is difficult to detect prior to use, CropSure Pty Ltd accepts no liability for any losses that may result from the failure of CropSure Jagged Selective Herbicide to control resistant weeds.
